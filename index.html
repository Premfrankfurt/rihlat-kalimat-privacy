Privacy Policy — رحلة الكلمات (Rihlat al-Kalimat: Arabic Word Puzzle)

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the game رحلة الكلمات (Rihlat al-Kalimat, "the game", "we") uses. It is short and plain on purpose. An Arabic version follows the English one.

Developer / contact: Premkumar Mangalaraman — developerpmk@gmail.com

1. The short version

  • You do not need an account. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or address.
  • Your progress (the puzzle you are on, your coins, the words you found, your sound setting) is saved only on your device.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob and offers optional coin packs through Google Play. Those companies handle some data, as explained below.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools of our own.

2. Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress, coins, found words and settings in the game's private storage on your device. We do not receive this information. If you uninstall the game, it is deleted.

3. Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads using Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect and use information such as:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Advertising ID);
  • your IP address and approximate location (for example, your country);
  • device and app information (device model, operating system, app version);
  • information about how you interact with ads.

This is used to show ads, measure how they perform and prevent fraud. You can control it on Android under Settings → Google → Ads (Delete advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalization).

Google's policies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads and https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Purchases

The game offers optional coin packs. Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your card or payment details; we receive only a confirmation that a purchase was completed, so that we can give you the coins.

5. Pictures

The place pictures in the game were created with AI image tools. They contain no personal information.

6. Children

The game is for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. The only parties who receive data because of the game are the services named above (Google AdMob and Google Play).

8. Your choices and rights

Because we do not collect personal information ourselves, there is nothing for us to delete or export. For data held by Google, use your Google account and ad settings. You may contact us with any privacy question.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top.

10. Contact

Premkumar Mangalaraman — developerpmk@gmail.com
